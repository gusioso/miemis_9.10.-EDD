## What to borrow
1. **Explainer-graphic shots between character scenes**, a new idea every 2-4 s:
   - sound waveform with a dashed box popping around one spike;
   - X-ray / scan HUD: teal glow, corner labels ("PATIENT // SCAN" style), scan line sweeping down, a growing highlight,
     data text typing in;
   - calendar with one date circled and a red chip ("5 WEEKS");
   - handwritten notebook whose lines type themselves in;
   - line graph that draws itself, then drops, the drop circled;
   - network diagram: one document in the centre linked to icons (doctors, hospital, records).
2. **Colour mood per shot** + heavy vignette: dark navy (tension), teal (tech/medical), warm sepia (rooms),
   yellow halftone (memories/flashbacks).
3. **Transitions**: VHS rewind (horizontal streaks + ⏪ icon) into flashbacks, then a "3 WEEKS AGO" pill;
   blur-dissolves between graphics; hard cuts elsewhere.
4. **Split screen** (cool blue side vs warm spotlight side) with a countdown chip ticking ("5 WK → 1 WK").
5. **Animated counters/chips** ("PAGES 21 → 38 → 40") with lighting changing night → morning.
6. **Short speech bubbles with real dialogue** in conversation scenes (only for fiction, never invented "quotes" for real people).
7. Small stick figures in big lit sets; graphics and lighting carry the story.
8. End on a cliffhanger line for the next part.
